# Viva Presentation — Overall Structure

**Format:** 10 minutes + 10-15 min Q&A
**Platform:** Microsoft Teams
**All 6 members must present**

## Narrative Flow

| # | Section | Speaker | Time | Handoff |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Opening + Storyboard | Charlotte Yu | ~1 min | "But to make this work, we faced three challenges." |
| 2 | System Architecture | Charlotte Yu | ~30 sec | "The first challenge was in the data." |
| 3 | Challenge 1: No fault labels | Lei Pei / Qiuting Fu | ~1.5 min | "With clean, structured data, we could now detect anomalies — but that brought its own challenge." |
| 4 | Challenge 2: Anomaly detection without ground truth | Ray Wang / Lucca Zhou | ~1.5 min | "We had a risk score — but a number means nothing to a car owner." |
| 5 | Challenge 3: Grounded language generation | Charlotte Yu / Jintong He | ~2 min | "So how do we know our reports are actually accurate?" |
| 6 | Evaluation | All groups | ~1.5 min | — |
| 7 | Live Demo | Charlotte Yu | ~1 min | — |
| 8 | Conclusion + Future Work | Charlotte Yu | ~30 sec | — |

## Key Principles (from supervisor feedback)

- Open with a storyboard animation
  showing how a car owner uses
  Granite Lifeline (animation TBD,
  Sprint 5)
- Use video to demonstrate the Dashboard
- Use diagrams and visualisations —
  not bullet points
- Every challenge must explain:
  why it is specific to this project,
  not a generic difficulty
- Each section must have a clear
  transition sentence to the next
